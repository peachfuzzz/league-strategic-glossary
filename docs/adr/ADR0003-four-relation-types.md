Choosing to support four relation types: 
- Broad/narrow
- Part/whole
- Dependent
- Related

## Problem

Links between terms don't convey information beyond "these ideas are mention each other in the text." This made the Graph view mostly uninformative. Connections do not say anything interesting about how the manner in which ideas are related.

## Rationale
- Broad/narrow is useful for specificity. Directioned
- Part/whole is useful for decomposition of a larger idea. Directioned
- Dependent is useful for showing "fundamentalness." Directioned
- Related is basically a note from me to the reader for interest. No direction

## Pros
- more information conveyed per connection
- graph view more representative of the theoretical structure of League

## Cons
- most links will now be manual
- likely will result in fewer overall connections
