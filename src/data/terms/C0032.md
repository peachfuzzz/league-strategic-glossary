A [[play]] where a [[C0059|player]] enters a lane unexpectedly to to help their ally, usually with the goal of killing the enemy laner(s). A jungler’s main way of influencing lanes is through ganking, though ganking can be performed by anyone outside of the ganked lane. The term gank mostly applies during lane phase, since lane assignments are much less strict later. Gank appears to be a portmanteau of “gang kill,” originating from MMOs.
