A window of time that is opportune for a recall. Recalls incur a high [[C0086|tempo]] cost. A recalling [[C0059|player]] is inactionable for a recall’s 8-second channel time, then off the map from any standing resources or potential [[C0058|plays]] until they leave base. An optimal recall timing aims to minimize tempo costs by finding a period of time where minimal enemy minions die and action on the map is low.
