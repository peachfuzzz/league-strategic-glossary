The route on which a jungler clears jungle camps, [[C0032|ganks]] lanes, and [[C0017|contests]] neutrals. Jungle pathing also encompasses the skill of planning such a route and the decision-making process of deciding where to go and when. “Pathing toward” is sometimes used in a similar context as the prioritization of an area of the map that a jungler paths towards, e.g. “pathing towards bot.”
