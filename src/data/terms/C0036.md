A champion with a kit that scales particularly well into the late game. The specific attributes that determine a hypercarry are up for debate, but generally hypercarries have high, reliable, target-agnostic damage with lots of multiplicative scalings. Marksmen make up the majority of hypercarry candidates, as basic attacks are very [[C0066|reliable]] and already have several multiplicative scalers in attack speed, attack damage, and crit chance.
