A state of raised stakes, especially with respect to the opposing team. Tension appears when two competing interests from opposing [[C0059|players]] collide, even without active [[C0016|combat]]. The presence of tension accentuates losses and gains—for each interest fulfilled by one side, an equivalent interest is lost for the other. Tension scales with both [[C0091|variance]] and [[C0090|value]]. The greater the variance in outcome or the greater the value of the outcome, the higher the tension rises.
