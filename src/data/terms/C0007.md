When a [[C0059|player]] purposefully puts themselves in a dangerous position in an attempt to get the opponent to take an action. The act of baiting can be done on multiple scales. On a [[C0072|skirmishing]] level, a player can bait to try to get an opponent to start a [[C0028|fight]], only to have their teammate(s) join once the opponents have committed to the fight. On a smaller scale, weaving in and out of the maximum range of an opponent's ability can maximize the chances of their opponent wasting a skillshot.
