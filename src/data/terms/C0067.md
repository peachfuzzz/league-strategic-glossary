Leaving a lane to make a [[C0058|play]]. Roaming lets a laner affect other parts of the map beyond their lane. Because a [[C0059|player]] is not present in lane during a roam to catch a [[C0092|wave]] or stop their opponent from hitting their tower, roams come with an opportunity cost. Setting up a roam requires the laner to account for this potential minion loss, such as [[C0020|crashing]] a [[C0073|slow push]] or (as a support) warding for an ally.
