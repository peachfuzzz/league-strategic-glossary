[[C0088|Threatening]] or [[C0028|fighting]] over something. A [[C0059|player]] can contest anything: a minion, lane priority, neutral objectives, etc. Contesting is a demonstration of intent and does not necessarily mean a full fight must happen. Even just the threat of fighting can be enough to achieve the goal of a contest.
