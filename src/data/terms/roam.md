Leaving a lane for an extended period of time. Roaming lets a laner affect other parts of the map beyond their lane. Because a player is not present in lane during a roam to catch a wave or stop their opponent from hitting their tower, roams come with an opportunity cost. Setting up a roam requires the laner to account for this potential minion loss, such as crashing a slow push or (as a support) warding for an ally.
