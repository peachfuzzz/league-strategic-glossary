When a [[C0059|player]] is in the opposing team’s area of map control. Due to the distance from their allies and the proximity of the opposing team, an overextending player is more vulnerable to a [[C0032|gank]] or [[C0015|collapse]]. Overextension is not necessarily a mistake, as it can [[C0007|bait]] the opposing team or apply map [[C0062|pressure]].
