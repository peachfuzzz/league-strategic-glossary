Recalling after crashing the first three minion waves of the game in a manner such that minimal minions are lost upon returning to lane without the assistance of teleport. While technically performable in bot lane, the cheater recall is most frequently used in top lane. A cheater recall is a reliable strategy for creating a lead after obtaining level one priority. The recall grants the player full resources, as well as the ability to spend their gold on a component for a combat advantage. The common follow-up on a cheater recall is to return to lane before the opposing wave crashes and to leverage their increased resources to hold a freeze.
