A significant increase in power. While not strictly defined, spikes generally refer to power increases that unlock new capabilities for a champion. Spikes that are commonly discussed include level 2, level 3, level 6, and completed items. Skilled players often formulate their gameplans around spikes, since they are a clear reference point to play around and can be used to surprise opponents.
