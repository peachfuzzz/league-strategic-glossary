Keeping a larger enemy minion [[C0092|wave]] near, but not within range of, an allied tower. A freeze forms when a large enemy wave can kill an allied wave before the allied minions can build up fast enough to reverse the [[C0093|wave direction]], while also not being allowed to [[C0020|crash]] into the allied tower. Freezing can allow a [[C0059|player]] to deny the opposing laner(s) of minion gold or even experience for an extended period of time, since the wave will not initiate a [[C0008|bounceback]] until the freeze is broken. A freeze breaks when the enemy minions cannot kill enough allied minions to prevent a buildup, and can happen intentionally or naturally. Within a large enough difference of ranged minions (usually 3+), a freeze can last indefinitely, sometimes referred to as a true freeze.
