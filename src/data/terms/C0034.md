The ladder ratings at which [[C0059|players]] are considered very skilled at the game. The exact threshold of high elo is a contentious topic among the community, with definitions of “high elo” ranging from Emerald+ to 1k LP Challenger. Riot’s balance framework, as of 2020, defines Elite play as Diamond 2 and onwards.
