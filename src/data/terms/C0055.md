As the bot laner or support, standing apart from their lane partner while maintaining approximately the same distance from the enemy laner(s). Parallel positioning aims to maximize [[C0074|space]] and [[C0088|threat]] while still ensuring neither the [[C0059|player]] nor their lane partner is the only vulnerable [[C0084|target]].
