When a [[C0092|wave]] of minions enters the opposing team’s tower range. Crashes are classified by “completeness,” determined by the location at which opposing waves meet relative to the tower. A crash is “full/complete” when all of the wave's minions directly attack the enemy tower. A crash is considered “partial” when some minions are not within tower range, resulting in a wave stalling just outside of tower range.

Crashes are strategically desirable because a crash and the following [[C0008|bounceback]] create a period of time in which few, if any, enemy minions die. A crash opens a window for an allied player to spend time outside of the lane—such as to [[C0067|roam]] or recall—without fear of losing excess gold and experience.
