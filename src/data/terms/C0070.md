The tactic of [[C0088|threatening]] enemy towers, which are actively defended by the opposing team, over an extended period of time. Sieges carry significant risk since if a [[C0028|fight]] occurs, the presence of the tower gives the defending team an advantage. Sieges are most commonly a midgame tactic, and their efficacy greatly increases if Baron is slain.
