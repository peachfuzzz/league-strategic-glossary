The phase of a [[C0092|minion wave]] that occurs after it [[C0020|crashes]] into the opposing team’s tower. During a bounceback, the opposing minions will outnumber the allied minions, forcing the wave to push back towards the allied tower. Bouncebacks are a period of higher [[C0087|tension]] during the lane phase because a death from either side results in more lost minions than an even wave. Bouncebacks can result in a [[C0031|freeze]].
