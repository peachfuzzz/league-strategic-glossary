The sequence of preparing a [[C0058|play]] around a neutral objective. The term objective setup can be used in specific, referring to a prepared, high-coordination sequence a team performs before an objective, or in general, referring to the general situation before a neutral objective is contested. The goal of an objective setup is to make the ensuing [[C0017|contest]] easier, through a mixture of meso and [[C0045|macro]] strategies.
