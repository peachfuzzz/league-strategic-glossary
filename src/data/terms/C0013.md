Recalling after [[C0020|crashing]] the first three [[C0092|minion waves]] of the game such that minimal minions are lost upon returning to lane. A cheater recall is a reliable strategy for creating a lead after obtaining level one priority. The recall grants the [[C0059|player]] full resources, as well as the ability to spend their gold on items for a [[C0016|combat]] advantage. The common follow-up on a cheater recall is to return to lane, without using teleport, before the opposing wave [[C0020|crashes]]. This allows the player to leverage their combat advantage, often to create a [[C0031|freeze]]. While technically performable in bot lane, the cheater recall is most frequently used in top lane.
