An unexpected decision by a [[C0059|player]], generally considered suboptimal, whose success relies on surprising the [[C0084|target]]. Picking off-meta champions can often be categorized as cheese, since a significant advantage can be gained from the information asymmetry of an unusual pick. [[C0058|Plays]] are also sometimes labeled as cheesy if the play would only work through the element of surprise.
