The state of being able to move from a lane before the opposing laner(s). Lane priority stems from the ability to push a [[C0092|wave]] in a manner such that the opposing laner cannot [[C0017|contest]]. A player with lane priority incurs fewer minion losses if they were to leave lane, giving them the opportunity of first move over their opponent to join [[C0058|plays]] around the map. Lane priority is a relative comparison of several factors, including [[C0040|kill pressure]], waveclear, and jungle proximity.
