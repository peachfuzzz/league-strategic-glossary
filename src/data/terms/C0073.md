Killing a [[C0092|minion wave]] as slowly as possible. Slow pushing is a medium-term wave tactic primarily used when a player has a longer window for manipulating a wave. Slow pushing is a common default tactic because minions naturally tend to move slowly if left undisturbed. Slow pushes happen over 2-4 waves and result in a large buildup of minions before eventually [[C0020|crashing]]. The eventual crash gives the player a timer to make a [[C0058|play]], such as a [[C0023|dive]], [[C0067|roam]], or recall.
