The label for any champion with a strong lane phase relative to other champions in their role. Lane bullies more easily win lane through direct [[C0016|combat]], whether through strong [[C0089|trades]] or [[C0003|all-ins]]. Lane bullies are contextual: the specific lane matchup can swing which champion(s) are considered the bully.
