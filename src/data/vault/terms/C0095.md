Killing a few minions in a [[C0092|wave]] without eliminating the entire wave. Thinning a wave is used to slow down a push or lower the possible incoming minion damage while preserving the [[C0093|wave direction]]. Thinning a wave is commonly performed to maintain a [[C0031|freeze]], [[C0017|contest]] a [[C0020|crash]], or stall a [[C0023|dive]].
