A [[C0028|fight]] between opposing [[C0059|players]] during lane that does not result in a kill. Trades are often evaluated based on the parity of resources exchanged. Trades can be categorized as short or long depending on the length of the fight. Sufficient trading can generate [[C0040|kill pressure]] or lead to an [[C0003|all-in]]
