Pushing a sidelane while split from the team. A [[C0079|splitpusher]] [[C0062|pressures]] the opposing team to send one or more [[C0059|players]] to respond, giving the splitpusher’s allies time to make a [[C0058|play]] on the opposite side of the map. Splitpushing as a strategy is often contrasted with [[C0085|teamfighting]].
