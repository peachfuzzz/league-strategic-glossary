A lane that receives comparatively more [[C0032|ganks]] from their teammates. Designating a lane as a team’s strongside (strongsiding) incentivizes the strongsided player to play more aggressively, since they are more likely to receive assistance with their [[C0058|plays]]. Strongsiding can help swing a volatile [[C0046|matchup]] or snowball an already advantaged lane at the cost of creating a [[C0096|weakside]].
