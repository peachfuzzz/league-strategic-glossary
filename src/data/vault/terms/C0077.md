A game state where both junglers opt to [[C0056|path]] on one side (bot/top) of the map only. Split maps often form as a result of vertical jungling, creating a well-defined [[C0081|strongside]] and [[C0096|weakside]]. Because split maps incentivize junglers to focus on only one side of the map while ignoring the other, side lanes that benefit from increased jungle presence benefit more from a successful split map.
