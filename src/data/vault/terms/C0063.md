Farming the enemy [[C0092|minion wave]] between standing enemy turrets. Proxying is a strategy aimed at minimizing interaction in lane. Since the proxying player kills the enemy minion waves before it can meet the allied wave, the allied wave will [[C0020|crash]] without interruption unless the opposing laner interferes. This creates a tempo advantage for the proxying player and prevents the opposing laner from pushing with their own wave for tower damage. In matchups with a particularly high disparity in waveclear, proxying can pin the lower waveclear laner under their tower while the proxying player can farm uncontested.
