A situation where two opposing [[C0059|players]] have discrete positive and negative outcomes and each attempts to [[C0007|bait]] the other into making a mistake. Mindgames primarily focus on prediction and guesswork rather than reaction-based execution. A common example of a mindgame involves a specific [[C0037|engine interaction]] between two abilities that counter each other—e.g. Sion’s Q versus Fiora’s W—where each player wants to use their ability to negate the other’s.
