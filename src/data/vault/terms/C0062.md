An active [[C0088|threat]] that incentivizes a response from the opponent. A [[C0059|player]] with pressure threatens the enemy to respond, and if left unanswered will create an advantage. Pressure punishes inaction and increases [[C0087|tension]]; under pressure, players are more likely to make uncharacteristic decisions and actions. Pressure can be applied at all game scales. 

Example: a laner applies pressure to their opponent by denying minions, a splitpusher applies pressure to the map by pushing waves, and a team applies pressure to the enemy by starting a neutral objective.
