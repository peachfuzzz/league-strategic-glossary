The [[C0062|pressure]] created when a [[C0059|player]] has [[C0044|lethal]] on a [[C0084|target]]. A player with kill pressure is afforded more freedom in decision-making, since their target risks their life if they attempt to [[C0017|contest]]. Kill pressure is most frequently discussed in the context of the isolated lane phase.
