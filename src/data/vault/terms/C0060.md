A champion that excels at creating advantageous situations for their team (making [[C0058|plays]]). Champions considered playmakers commonly have reliable crowd-control which they use to start a [[C0028|fight]], but rely on their teammates to deal damage and finish a fight. The general capability of making plays is called “playmaking.”
