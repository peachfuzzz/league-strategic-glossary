A proactive and committal sequence intended to achieve a goal. A play refers the intent of a player to take action, not the sequence of events that unfolds in-game. A play begins when a player commits to a sequence and ends when the play's goal is deemed to be fulfilled or not. A play can happen over anything and does not necessarily need to involve [[C0016|combat]].
