Intentionally staying within a specific range of a [[C0084|target]]. Spacing maintains [[C0088|threat]] on an enemy while staying out of range of an enemy’s response. Most often, a [[C0059|player]] will be spacing a specific [[C0039|key ability]] or combination of abilities.
