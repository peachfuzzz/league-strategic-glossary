A [[C0058|play]] in which a [[C0059|player]] directly threatens the opposing team over a specific thing. Anything can be the target of a contest: a minion, lane priority, neutral objectives, etc. A contest is a demonstration of intent and does not necessarily mean a full [[C0028|fight]] must happen. Even just the threat of fighting can be enough to achieve the goal of a contest.
