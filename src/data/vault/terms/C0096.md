A lane that receives comparatively fewer [[C0032|ganks]] from its allies. Designating a lane as a team’s weakside (weaksiding) incentivizes the weaksided [[C0059|player(s)]] to play more cautiously, since they are less likely to receive assistance against enemy [[C0058|plays]]. A weakside is the cost of a strongside: investing heavily in one lane means leaving another to fend for themselves.
