A [[C0028|fight]] between opposing players with the intention to kill. An all-in is taken either when a [[C0059|player]] has sufficient [[C0040|kill pressure]], either via a calculation of [[C0044|lethal]], or as a natural consequence of prior [[C0016|combat]].  All-ins often expend every resource and cooldown available to all champions involved.
