A situation when a [[C0059|player]] believes they have sufficient damage to kill a [[C0084|target]]. Lethal is a speculative bet, not a deterministic state. Having lethal does not mean that a player can do anything and come away with a kill, but that a player with lethal has identified a specific sequence that [[C0066|reliably]] kills their target.
