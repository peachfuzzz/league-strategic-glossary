Actively choosing not to [[C0017|contest]] something. Anything can be given: a jungle camp, [[C0074|space]], a [[C0092|minion wave]], etc. Giving is not necessarily a passive decision, and can be done strategically to allocate time and resources towards a more valuable play. A common reason to give is to set up a [[C0021|crossmap]].
