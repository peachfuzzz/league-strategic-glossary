Allowing the enemy to make a [[C0058|play]]. A [[C0059|player]] can give anything: a jungle camp, space, towers, etc. Giving is not necessarily a passive decision, and can be done strategically to allocate time and resources towards a more valuable play. A common reason to give is to set up a [[C0021|crossmap]].
